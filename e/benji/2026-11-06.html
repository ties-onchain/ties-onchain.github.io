How events are classified

Subscription
A mint outside the nightly dividend run.
Dividend
A mint inside the nightly run (about 1,000 single mints on Stellar), one per holder per night.
Redemption
A burn, or on Stellar tokens sent back to the issuer.
Chain move
A burn on one chain and the same amount minted on another within minutes, or the issuer's own cross-chain label. Nets to zero for the fund.
Unclassified
Touches the supply but fits no rule. Listed, never dropped.

Rules can improve; an event keeps its link when its label changes. This day as JSON.
