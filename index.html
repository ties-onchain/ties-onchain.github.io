How many fund tokens exist right now?

Tokenized money market funds and Treasury products live on many chains at once. ties reads each contract every night, from two independent providers, at finalized state, and adds it up. Token counts only: no prices, no NAV.

For teams that lend against or hold these funds: every BUIDL and BENJI mint and burn is classified as a subscription, redemption or chain move, and reconciled to supply each night. Want an alert on a fund? hello@tiesonchain.com

How it is read

EVM chains
totalSupply() at the finalized block.
Solana
Mint supply at finalized commitment.
Aptos
Fungible asset supply, pinned to one ledger version.
Stellar
Every trustline, claimable balance, pool and contract amount of the asset, from Horizon.
Stellar contracts
TotalSupply from the token contract's own storage, via Soroban RPC.
Starknet
total_supply() at one block.
Sui, Noble, NEAR
Coin supply, bank supply and ft_total_supply(), at the latest final state.
XRP Ledger
The issuer's obligations (everything held on trustlines) at a validated ledger.

A contract counts as confirmed only when two independent providers return the same figure to the cent. Anything else is shown as it is, never smoothed over. Contract addresses come from public token lists and are checked against the name each contract reports on-chain.

Every daily read is archived at a permanent address (data/<fund>/<day>.json) with each provider's figure and the block or ledger it was read at, so a number can be cited and checked later.

ties is independent and not affiliated with BlackRock, Franklin Templeton, Spiko, Ondo, Circle, Janus Henderson or any issuer named here. It has no token, sells nothing and is paid by no one it covers. It is read-only public data, not investment advice.
