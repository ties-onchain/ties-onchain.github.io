7 tokenized funds, added up across 22 chains every night.

ties reads each of their 55 contracts from two independent nodes and puts the total next to CoinGecko and, where one exists, the fund's own SEC filing. Token counts only: no prices, no NAV.

Exceptions in the latest read

What does not simply add up, by type. A mismatch means two public numbers differ, not which one is right.

  • Source mismatch

    Franklin BENJI: CoinGecko counts 6 of 8 chains

    Its total supply, 67,316,101.44, equals Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Avalanche, Polygon and Solana added up, to the hundredth of a token. Left out: Stellar and Aptos, 1.31B tokens, 95% of the fund.

  • Source mismatch

    Janus Henderson JTRSY: CoinGecko counts 1 of 8 chains

    Its total supply, 22,196,750.14, equals Ethereum alone, to the hundredth of a token. Left out: Base, Stellar, Monad, Plume, BNB Chain, Arbitrum and Avalanche, 13.7M tokens, 38% of the fund.

  • Possible double count

    Janus Henderson JTRSY: Stellar supply may be counted twice

    One Ethereum address (0x528d86…faF25d) holds 4,547,091.59 tokens, equal to the whole Stellar supply to the hundredth of a token. The Stellar tokens may mirror tokens held on Ethereum; if so, 4.5M tokens (12.7% of the fund) are counted twice in the total. Not yet confirmed with the issuer.

  • Known limit

    Ondo USDY: Sui is read from one node, not two

    Sui retired public JSON-RPC; graphql.mainnet.sui.io is the only free endpoint (checked 2026-11-20: Mysten mirror and BlockVision refuse). The figure (10,813,796.99) is counted and shown, never marked confirmed.

  • Known gap

    BlackRock BUIDL: BNB Chain events missing for 3 days

    2026-11-16 to 2026-11-18: archived as empty by a node that had pruned its logs (supply moved, events 0); no free BNB Chain log source keeps that day (checked 2026-11-20). Supply on those days is read; the individual mints and burns are not.

Everything else reconciles: 54 of 55 contracts confirmed by two independent nodes; Franklin BENJI is about +0.03% from its SEC filing (31 Oct).

Not listed here: 1 weak bridge match (a large holder near another chain's supply, but not to the hundredth of a token), shown on the fund page with the reasoning.

BENJI on-chain matches its SEC filing to within 0.05%

Shares outstanding reported to the SEC (N-MFP3): 667,967,346.67 on 30 Sep and 833,590,155.07 on 31 Oct.
