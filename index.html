How it is read

EVM chains
totalSupply() at the finalized block.
Solana
Mint supply at finalized commitment.
Aptos
Fungible asset supply, pinned to one ledger version.
Stellar
Every trustline, claimable balance, pool and contract amount of the asset, from Horizon.

A contract counts as confirmed only when two independent providers return the same figure to the cent. Anything else is shown as it is, never smoothed over. Contract addresses come from public token lists and are checked against the name each contract reports on-chain.

ties has no token and sells nothing. It is read-only public data, not investment advice.
